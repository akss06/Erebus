Erébus: blind labelling

Radar images from the Gulf of Mannar. For each one, decide whether the object in the red circle looks like a ship.

How to judge
  • Looks like a ship: a compact bright spot, maybe slightly stretched. Big ships sometimes have a faint bright cross or line through them (radar glare).
  • Not a ship: a long thin streak or line, part of a coastline or reef, a fuzzy patch, or speckle barely brighter than the sea.
  • Can't tell: anything in between. This is a valid answer; use it honestly.

Each image is about 1.2 km across. Judge only what you see. Don't look anything up or discuss with teammates until everyone has finished. Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can close the page and come back. Keyboard: 1 = ship, 2 = not a ship, 3 = can't tell, ← = back.
